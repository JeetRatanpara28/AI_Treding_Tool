**Title:** Reproducible, cost-aware evaluation of daily NSE cash-equity paper strategies

**Objective and relevance:**

Evaluate a fixed daily equity research baseline against comparable buy-and-hold
benchmarks, with explicit transaction-cost/slippage assumptions and controls for
future-information leakage, selection bias and corporate actions. The project is
local academic/personal research with INR 0 real capital, no broker login, no
real orders and no paid service. Please confirm whether the proposed use meets
the programme's academic/non-commercial conditions; eligibility is not assumed.

**Data type requested:** Daily reports and historical daily cash-equity/index
data; no tick-by-tick feed, derivatives, intraday data or personal trading records.

**Period:** 07/11/2021 through 06/11/2026 inclusive. The first 200 valid sessions
will be used for indicator warm-up, so the evaluated performance period will be
shorter than five years. A full five-year evaluation would require separately
confirmed earlier warm-up history, rather than silently extending this request.

**Instruments:** NSE EQ-series RELIANCE, TCS, HDFCBANK, HINDUNILVR and LT, plus
Nifty 50 daily price-index values. This is a small engineering pilot, not a
claim of a representative or survivorship-free historical investable universe.

**Fields and supporting records:** Session date, symbol, ISIN/identity/series,
OHLC, traded volume; corporate actions (including effective dates and ratios),
symbol/ISIN changes, applicable historical session calendars and format notes.
For the index: daily OHLC and its price-versus-total-return convention. If a
total-return comparison is required, please identify the appropriate data and
permitted access separately. No personal identifying market-participant data.

**Justification:** A small daily sample is enough to validate the pipeline before
expansion. Missing closes, schema changes, splits/bonuses/mergers and calendar
gaps can otherwise masquerade as strategy performance. We need source identity,
consistent price basis and documented coverage before evaluating returns.

**Access and use clarification requested:**

Please identify an applicable no-cost route and clarify whether we may retrieve
the approved files programmatically, retain original bytes privately on a local
SSD, normalize them into separate local tables, and run local automated
historical backtests. Please state rate/volume limits, attribution, retention,
publication and any agreement requirements. We will not redistribute raw data.
Later AI/ML experiments are not part of this initial request; please clarify
whether they would need separate permission. If the academic programme is not
applicable to this individual project, please advise whether any free personal
research access route covers these activities.

**Publication:** Any later publication would be limited to permitted aggregate
research findings; raw data, private ledgers and personal documents remain private.
