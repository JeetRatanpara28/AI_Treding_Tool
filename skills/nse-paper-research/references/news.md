# Public news, fundamentals and event timing

## Source hierarchy and access

Use original exchange-hosted issuer announcements, financial results and actions
(N1-N3); RBI for monetary policy (N4); SEBI/exchange circulars for current rules.
Company investor-relations documents add context. Reporting can identify a topic,
but syndicated headlines are not independent confirmations. Official hosting
identifies publication provenance, not the truth of every issuer assertion.

Public-news permission is not access to private Gmail/logged-in accounts. No
login, paid feed or background subscription is needed. If dynamic rows or an
attachment cannot be read, report that limit; never invent today's news summary.

## Repeatable manual review

When requested, identify date range, watchlist, cutoff and permissions. Before a
research session review scheduled results/meetings, actions and macro events;
at the chosen checkpoint verify new filings; after close reconcile corrections
and next-session events. This workflow does not create an automatic polling job.

Record issuer, symbol/ISIN, event type, original URL/document ID/hash, public
publication/dissemination time, observation time, effective/ex-date, timezone,
version and verified facts. Distinguish issuer transaction date, exchange receipt,
public dissemination, record date and publication date.

Historical availability requires the original archived version and verified
public timestamp. Without those, availability is no earlier than observation;
do not backdate today's revised attachment. Receipt is not automatically public
dissemination. Convert IST/UTC explicitly, deduplicate cross-posts and preserve
corrections as versions. Never put revised results or later outcomes into an
earlier decision. Apply a stated execution/processing latency separately.

A release at Friday 18:00 IST is unavailable to that day's 15:30 decision. Find
the next eligible session from the actual calendar, not a hardcoded Monday.
The helper checks supplied timestamp order, not source truth or trade eligibility.

## Analyze mechanisms, not headline direction

| Event | Checks | Invalid shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Results | Consolidated/standalone, YoY/seasonality, revenue/margins, one-offs, operating cash flow, debt and guidance basis. | Profit growth automatically means buy; accounting profit always equals cash. |
| Contract/order | Signed scope, cancellations, tenure, revenue recognition, issuer scale and disclosures. | Headline order value equals immediate profit. |
| Governance/audit | Qualifications, related parties, promoter/pledge disclosures, litigation and corrections. | A rumor proves wrongdoing, or an official filing cannot be wrong. |
| Dividend/split/bonus/rights/demerger | Announcement, ex/record/payment dates and price/share-count treatment. | An unadjusted gap automatically signals a bargain or realized loss. |
| Rates/inflation/currency/commodities | Original release/vintage and plausible sector/cash-flow mechanism. | A macro surprise has one guaranteed direction for every stock. |
| Regulatory action | Entity, scope, operative date and later orders. | A headline supplies complete current legal/trading rules. |

Ratios need consistent dates, units and accounting basis; a low P/E is not a
thesis. Earnings surprise requires consensus known BEFORE release. If reliable
free consensus history is unavailable, mark the feature unavailable; do not
invent a consensus or access a paid account.

For each item distinguish fact/source/time, possible mechanism, contrary evidence
and uncertainty, then whether a pre-specified research rule is eligible, blocked
or unchanged. A sentiment score is not a probability of appreciation. LLM labels
require point-in-time inputs, evaluation, versioning and missing-data handling.
Never map arbitrary positive sentiment directly to an order.
